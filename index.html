About Me
I DON'T JUST CREATE CONTENT.

I'm Tisha — a Social Media Manager, Content Strategist and Creator. I work across strategy, content, shoots, publishing and performance, with a strong focus on turning social media into a measurable brand-growth channel.

I grew my own Instagram presence from 5,000 → 20,000+ followers organically, while creating and delivering 100+ UGC videos across brands, agencies and companies. My work has spanned immigration, travel, beauty, fashion, products, agencies, marketing, AI/tools and branding.

I'm hands-on from idea to execution: audience and competitor research, content pillars, calendars, scripts, captions, short-form video, on-camera content, shoots, publishing, community touchpoints and performance tracking. I care about both how content looks and what it does for the business.
